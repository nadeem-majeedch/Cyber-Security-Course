# Short Assignments (SA-1 … SA-6)

Individual written work, formative feedback, ~1,000 words each unless noted. Submit via the course platform. These briefs are the authoritative task statements.

| ID | Due | CLO | Task |
|---|---|---|---|
| SA-1 | End of Week 3 | 1 | **Threat model of a personal project.** STRIDE table (≥ 12 entries) for a system you built or use; rank top 3 risks; one control per risk. |
| SA-2 | End of Week 6 | 2 | **Remediation memo.** From Lab 05/06 findings: a one-page memo recommending fixes for two flaws, with effort/impact tradeoffs. |
| SA-3 | End of Week 9 | 3, 4 | **ATT&CK mapping.** Given the course PCAP: map 5 adversary behaviors to technique IDs with evidence lines. |
| SA-4 | End of Week 12 | 5 | **Crypto design review.** Review the given design snippet; identify 2 misuses (mode, KDF, or key storage) and justify replacements. |
| SA-5 | End of Week 14 | 6 | **Dataset honesty check.** Evaluate the given synthetic log dataset: 3 leakage risks and your corrected validation protocol. |
| SA-6 | End of Week 15 | 7 | **DPIA skeleton.** For the given campus deployment: necessity, proportionality, top 3 risks, mitigations. |

## Grading (formative — feeds the 15% checkpoint/participation component through checkpoints, not directly)

Each SA is marked ✓+/✓/✓− against: task completeness, evidence use, correctness of named concepts, clarity. ✓+ on 4 of 6 earns the full participation credit; ✓− twice triggers a check-in with the teaching team. SA feedback explicitly rehearses the checkpoint question style — treat them as low-stakes practice with real feedback.
