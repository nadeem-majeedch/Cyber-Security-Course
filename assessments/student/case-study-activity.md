# Case-Study Brief (Week 12 deliverable · 10% of final grade)

## Task

Write a **1,500-word structured brief** on **one** case from `case-studies/collection/` at Level 3 or above (your choice; register your selection in Week 11 to avoid duplicates — max 2 students per case).

The brief answers four questions in order, with these approximate word budgets:

1. **What happened and why does it matter?** (300) — Restate the scenario and its stakeholders in your own words. Show you understand the system before judging it.
2. **What is the core failure?** (350) — Name the concepts (CIA element, STRIDE element, ATT&CK technique, CWE, control gap) that explain the failure. Precision beats quantity.
3. **What would have prevented or detected it?** (500) — A control set with **traceability**: each control maps to the failure mode it addresses, with one cost or friction tradeoff acknowledged.
4. **What is the honest residual risk?** (350) — What your control set does not fix, who must accept it, and how it would be reviewed.

## Rules

- **Defensive framing only**: you analyze the scenario as given; no operational offense detail is expected or rewarded.
- Cite course materials and the case's own evidence list; external sources must be real and attributable (no invented statistics — if you cannot source a number, argue qualitatively).
- The **model solution is revealed in class after submission** — briefs submitted before the reveal are assessed on reasoning, not on matching it; briefs that merely restate the reveal lose the analysis marks.

## Rubric (summary — full version in class)

| Dimension | Weight | Top band looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Understanding | 20% | Accurate restatement; stakeholders and constraints visible |
| Analytical precision | 30% | Correct named concepts (CWE/ATT&CK/STRIDE) tied to specific evidence |
| Traceable remediation | 30% | Every control maps to a failure mode; tradeoffs acknowledged |
| Honest residual risk | 15% | Named gap, owner, review mechanism |
| Quality of writing | 5% | Structured, concise, no padding |

Level 4 cases earn a +5% difficulty bonus on the final brief mark (capped at 100).
