# Quiz 14 — Checkpoint F (Week 14, L28) · Module 7: Cloud & Privacy
**Graded formative · recorded /10 · 12 minutes · CLO-7 · Closed book.**

1. On a PaaS deployment, applying security patches to the *runtime* is whose responsibility — "of the cloud" or "in the cloud"? Name the responsible party. **[1]**

2. An IAM policy shows `Action: "*"` on `Resource: "*"`. What does this grant, and name one way such policies typically appear. **[1]**

3. Give **two** advantages of role-assumed credentials over static access keys. **[1]**

4. Which log type answers "who deleted the bucket" — data-plane or control-plane — and why in one sentence? **[1]**

5. A resources team finds a public bucket in a subscription where IaC declared it private. Name this condition, and give the pair of practices that prevent it. **[1]**

6. "Anonymized" records still contain rare combinations of postcode, birth date, and diagnosis. Give the correct term for this data state and its implication under GDPR-style rules. **[1]**

7. *(Scenario, 2 marks)* A web app runs on IaaS with managed Postgres. Allocate responsibility for patching, IAM, encryption keys, and audit logging across provider/customer, and justify the one genuinely *shared* item.

8. *(Scenario, 2 marks)* A user invokes erasure of their account data. Name two other rights you must verify in the same request, and the two data stores that most often block compliance.

*Scoring: 1 mark per numbered item; recorded as a fraction of 10.*
