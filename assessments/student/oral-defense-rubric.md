# Oral Defense & Presentation Rubric (Capstone defense · Week 16)

Applies to the capstone defense and the case-study group presentations. Full marks require more than correct content — the defense tests whether you can **defend a decision under pressure** and reason aloud about tradeoffs.

## Rubric (20 points → scaled to component weight)

| Dimension | Marks | Excellent (full) | Adequate (~60%) | Weak (<40%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Structure & delivery** | 4 | Clear arc: threat → control → detection → IR; slides support, never read; time kept | Mostly organized; some reading; minor time overrun | Disjointed; slides as script; far over/under time |
| **Technical accuracy** | 4 | Correct terminology (CWE/ATT&CK/primitives) used precisely; no unjustified claims | Minor imprecision; claims mostly supported | Wrong concepts; buzzwords without mechanism |
| **Reasoning & tradeoffs** | 4 | Every major choice shows the rejected alternative and the price paid | Tradeoffs named but thin | No alternatives; "best practice" as argument |
| **Defense under questioning** | 5 | Direct answers; concedes valid points and re-reasons; says "we don't know — here's how we'd find out" when true | Answers most questions; some deflection | Blocks, bluffs, or freezes; no recovery |
| **Individual accountability** | 3 | Every member answers solo questions coherently in their area | Most members contribute under questioning | One member answers everything |

## How questioning works

- The panel (instructor + TA) asks from the rubric dimensions: **why this control**, **what your detection misses**, **who accepts the residual risk**.
- Expect at least one *challenge* question where the panel argues a position against yours — the tested skill is updating reasoning, not holding ground.
- One question per member is guaranteed solo (Individual accountability marks).

## Conduct rules

- 10 minutes presentation, 10 minutes questions; timing is part of Structure & delivery.
- Demonstrations must run on the course sandbox only; any live evidence must be reproducible from your report's method section.
- If a team member cannot attend: documented absence → individual defense scheduled within 3 days; the team presents otherwise.

## What the panel is *not* testing

Not polish. Not marketing. Not exhaustive breadth — a narrow, defensible, traceable design beats a broad hand-wavy one, in marks and in practice.
